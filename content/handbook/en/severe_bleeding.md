# Severe Bleeding

> [!WARNING]
> **NEEDS MEDICAL REVIEW**
> This handbook contains baseline disaster-first-aid reference guidelines only. It is intended for emergency decision-support when emergency services are delayed. It does NOT substitute for professional medical care.

---

### Immediate Action Checklist

1. **Protect Yourself & Patient**
   - Wear protective gloves if available.
   - Have the injured person sit or lie down flat to reduce risk of fainting and shock.

2. **Apply Direct Pressure**
   - Place a clean sterile dressing, clean cloth, or garment directly over the wound.
   - Press down firmly with both hands using body weight.
   - Maintain continuous, uninterrupted pressure for at least 10–15 minutes.

3. **Do NOT Remove Saturated Cloths**
   - If blood seeps through, do NOT remove the first dressing.
   - Place additional cloths directly on top and continue pressing firmly.

4. **Embedded Objects**
   - If an object (glass, knife, metal) is stuck in the wound, **DO NOT REMOVE IT**.
   - Press firmly on both sides of the object to stem bleeding without pushing the object deeper.
   - Build up padding around the object to stabilize it.

5. **Treat for Shock**
   - Keep the person warm with a jacket, blanket, or foil emergency sheet.
   - Elevate legs slightly (15–30 cm) if there are no suspected spinal or leg fractures.
   - Do NOT give them food or water.

# Choking Relief (Adult & Child)

> [!WARNING]
> **NEEDS MEDICAL REVIEW**
> This handbook contains baseline disaster-first-aid reference guidelines only. It is intended for emergency decision-support when emergency services are delayed. It does NOT substitute for professional medical care.

---

### Immediate Action Checklist

1. **Assess Severity**
   - **Mild Obstruction**: Person can cough forcefully, speak, or cry.
     - *Action*: Encourage continuous coughing. Do NOT interfere or pat back if they are coughing strongly.
   - **Severe Obstruction**: Person cannot speak, cough, or breathe; holds hands to throat (universal choking sign); lips or face turning blue.
     - *Action*: Act immediately.

2. **Deliver 5 Sharp Back Blows**
   - Stand to the side and slightly behind the choking person.
   - Support their chest with one hand and lean them forward so the object falls outward.
   - Give up to **5 sharp blows between shoulder blades** with the heel of your free hand.

3. **Deliver 5 Abdominal Thrusts (Heimlich Maneuver)**
   - Stand directly behind the person and wrap arms around their upper waist.
   - Make a fist with one hand; place thumb side against person’s abdomen, slightly above navel and below ribcage.
   - Grasp fist with your other hand.
   - Pull sharply inward and upward in 5 quick, distinct thrusts.

4. **Repeat the 5-and-5 Cycle**
   - Alternate 5 back blows and 5 abdominal thrusts until:
     - The lodged foreign object is expelled and person breathes normally.
     - The person becomes unresponsive.

5. **If the Person Becomes Unresponsive**
   - Lower them gently to the ground onto their back.
   - Shout for an ambulance and AED immediately.
   - Begin CPR chest compressions (30 compressions).
   - Before giving rescue breaths, look inside mouth; if you see the object, sweep it out with a finger. Never perform blind finger sweeps.

# Not Breathing (Hands-Only CPR)

> [!WARNING]
> **NEEDS MEDICAL REVIEW**
> This handbook contains baseline disaster-first-aid reference guidelines only. It is intended for emergency decision-support when emergency services are delayed. It does NOT substitute for professional medical care.

---

### Immediate Action Checklist

1. **Check Responsiveness & Breathing**
   - Tap shoulders firmly and shout: *"Are you okay?"*
   - Check the chest for normal breathing for no more than 10 seconds.
   - Gasping or irregular snorts are **NOT** normal breathing.

2. **Call for Immediate Help**
   - Direct a bystander: *"Call emergency services and look for an AED."*
   - If alone, call first, put phone on speaker, and immediately begin compressions.

3. **Hand Placement**
   - Position person flat on their back on a firm, flat surface (floor or ground).
   - Place heel of one hand in the center of the chest (between nipples).
   - Place heel of other hand on top, interlacing fingers.

4. **Deliver Rapid, Hard Chest Compressions**
   - Lock elbows straight and position shoulders directly above hands.
   - Push hard and fast: compress at least 5–6 cm (2 inches) deep.
   - Maintain a rate of **100 to 120 compressions per minute** (match the tempo of "Stayin' Alive").
   - Allow chest to return to normal height completely between each push.

5. **Do Not Stop**
   - Continue uninterrupted compressions until:
     - Person shows obvious signs of life (wakes up, breathes normally).
     - Trained emergency responders arrive and relieve you.
     - You become physically exhausted (switch with another person every 2 minutes if possible).

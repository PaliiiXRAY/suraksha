# Burn & Scald Treatment

> [!WARNING]
> **NEEDS MEDICAL REVIEW**
> This handbook contains baseline disaster-first-aid reference guidelines only. It is intended for emergency decision-support when emergency services are delayed. It does NOT substitute for professional medical care.

---

### Immediate Action Checklist

1. **Stop the Burning Process**
   - Extinguish flames (Stop, Drop, and Roll) or break contact with heat source.
   - For chemical burns: brush dry chemicals off skin, then flush with large amounts of water.
   - For electrical burns: do NOT touch patient until power source is safely disconnected.

2. **Cool with Clean Running Water**
   - Cool the burn under gentle, running cool (room temperature) water for **10 to 20 minutes**.
   - Cooling limits tissue damage and substantially reduces pain.
   - **NEVER use ice, ice water, or freezing compresses** (freezing damages fragile skin cells).

3. **Remove Constrictive Items**
   - Remove rings, watches, tight clothing, or shoes near burn before area begins swelling.
   - **DO NOT pull away clothing that is melted or stuck to the burn**.

4. **Cover Cleanly**
   - Cover loosely with a sterile, non-adherent dressing or clean plastic wrap.
   - Do NOT wrap tightly.

5. **Things to Avoid**
   - **DO NOT apply butter, grease, toothpaste, turmeric, or oil** to burns.
   - **DO NOT break or puncture blisters** (blisters protect against infection).
   - Keep patient comfortable and warm, watching for shock.

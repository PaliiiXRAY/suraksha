# Fracture & Broken Bones

> [!WARNING]
> **NEEDS MEDICAL REVIEW**
> This handbook contains baseline disaster-first-aid reference guidelines only. It is intended for emergency decision-support when emergency services are delayed. It does NOT substitute for professional medical care.

---

### Immediate Action Checklist

1. **Recognize Signs of a Fracture**
   - Severe localized pain, tenderness, swelling, and rapid bruising.
   - Visible deformity or abnormal angle of the limb.
   - Inability or refusal to bear weight or move the extremity.
   - Grating sound (crepitus) or bone piercing through skin (open fracture).

2. **Keep the Limb Still & Supported**
   - Support the injured area above and below the suspected break.
   - **DO NOT attempt to straighten, realign, or push back any bone**.
   - Keep the injured person still; prevent movement of neck, spine, or pelvis if serious trauma occurred.

3. **Immobilize with a Splint (If Transportation Is Needed)**
   - Use rigid materials (rolled magazines, wood slats, straight umbrella) padded with towels or clothing.
   - Splint in the exact position found.
   - Secure above and below the fracture site with strips of cloth or bandages.
   - Ensure ties are snug but NOT cutting off blood circulation.

4. **Manage Swelling & Open Wounds**
   - If bone pierced skin: cover wound gently with a sterile dressing; apply gentle pressure around edges only to control bleeding.
   - Apply an ice pack wrapped in a cloth (never ice directly on bare skin) for 15-minute intervals.

5. **Monitor Circulation**
   - Check fingers or toes beyond the injury site every 15 minutes.
   - If digits become cold, pale, blue, or numb, loosen splint bandages immediately.
